# Parallel

- Scope: 197 addresses of Parallel Protocol V3 on about 20 EVM chains (Ethereum,
  Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Polygon, BNB, Avalanche, Sonic, HyperEVM, Sei,
  Berachain, Gnosis, Scroll, Unichain, Linea, Katana, Fraxtal, World Chain,
  Plasma...): Parallelizer (diamond facets), Savings (sUSDp), Core (TokenP,
  USDp, ParallelAccessManager), Bridging (BridgeableTokenP, lz-USDp),
  Flashloan, PRL (PRL, sPRL1/2, PeripheralPRL, Lockbox, fee distributors).
- Copied at their default branches (main; no deployment tags): the five
  repositories the page links - parallel-parallelizer (BUSL-1.1),
  parallel-tokens (the page's "parrallel-tokens" redirects to it),
  parallel-core, parallel-prl, parallel-tokenomics (MIT).
- Left out: parallel-protocol/bridging-module (older BridgeableTokenP, not
  linked, superseded by parallel-tokens), layerzero-wire (TypeScript config),
  the app (website).
