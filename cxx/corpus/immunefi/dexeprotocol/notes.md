# DeXe Protocol

- Scope: 10 addresses on BNB Chain: a DeXe DAO (GovPool), ContractsRegistry,
  UserRegistry, CoreProperties, PriceFeed, ERC721Expert, PoolFactory,
  PoolRegistry, SphereXEngine and PoolSphereXEngine.
- Copied: dexe-network/DeXe-Protocol @master (its default branch; the page
  links its audits README), MIT. All names above are in contracts/, except
  SphereXEngine (the @spherex-xyz/engine-contracts npm package, copied under
  node_modules/) and PoolSphereXEngine (presumably a second SphereXEngine).
- programs.json's languages (Go, Python, TypeScript) are page tags; the
  contracts are Solidity.
