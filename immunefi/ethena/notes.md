# Ethena

- Scope: 18 EVM addresses on Ethereum and Mantle (USDe, EthenaMinting V2,
  StakedUSDe, USDeSilo, SingleAdminAccessControl, EthenaLPStaking,
  StakingRewardsDistributor, ENA, StakedENA, USDtb, USDtbMinting, the USDtb
  PSM, LayerZero OFT adapters and OFTs for USDe/sUSDe/ENA), 4 TON contracts
  (not EVM) and websites.
- Copied at default branches: ethena-labs/bbp-public-assets (the page's
  bug-bounty sources: USDe, minting, staking, silo, LP staking, ENA) and
  ethena-labs/ethena-usdtb-contest (USDtb, USDtbMinting; found in the org).
  Neither has a license file: only sources with open SPDX headers are copied.
- Not found in a public repository: StakedENA, the USDtb PSM, the OFT
  adapters/OFTs (LayerZero standard contracts, Etherscan only).
- Left out: the TON contracts.
